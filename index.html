How it works

Five steps, from the first call to the final walk-through

You do not need drawings or a budget to call us. Here is what happens and what you decide at each step.

01

Free call

Tell us which room is not working and roughly what you hope to spend. In 20 minutes we tell you if the project is realistic for that budget.

02

Home visit

We measure the room and watch how your family uses it. Within a week you get a floor plan to review, and we change it until it fits.

03

Fixed price

You choose each finish, from cabinets to tile, at one of three price levels. We send a line-by-line quote with a fixed total and a start date. Nothing starts until you sign it.

04

The work

One project lead runs the site and sends you a written update with photos every Friday. If something changes, you see the cost and the delay before you say yes.

05

Walk-through

We walk the room with you and write down anything that is not right. Every item on that list gets a date, and the last payment waits until it is done.
